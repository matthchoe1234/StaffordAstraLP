## The page’s structure

The reference uses a campaign-led sequence: attention, athlete credibility, product education, product choice, and scientific support. Its first shopping choices appear well below the opening campaign content.

| Section | Observed composition | Purpose | Graymatter adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Announcement and navigation | Slim announcement, left logo, outlined pill navigation | Establish brand and retain access to shopping | Graymatter wordmark with a compact pill navigation |
| Campaign media | Full-width embedded video with a monochrome interview thumbnail | Lead with a recognisable person and a story | Monochrome Rayshawn image beside an oversized campaign headline; no invented video control |
| Partner introduction | Equal-width copy and hand-held product image | Connect the person to a daily product routine | Product introduction beside original Graymatter tabletop photography |
| Athlete testimony | Circular/scalloped portrait beside a large quotation | Make the partnership personal | Circular Rayshawn portrait and clearly editorial copy, pending a verified quote |
| Ingredient benefits | Three white image cards on a colored background | Make the formula easier to understand | Three cards on Powder Blue, with local expandable ingredient text |
| Product choice | Four repeated product cards with prominent pill buttons | Give a simple route to purchase | Three flavor cards using actual Graymatter pack cutouts |
| Technology explanation | Large image beside ruled explanatory rows | Give a reason to believe | Formulation and quality information beside existing campaign photography |
| Expert credibility | Named expert portraits in a horizontal carousel | Add human authority | Consolidated into factual formulation content; no advisory board invented |
| Footer | Brand, newsletter, navigation, support, disclosure | Provide the next step and essential information | Final flavor CTA, real support links, disclaimer, and local-preview label |

## Visual construction

**Rhythm.** The reference alternates generous two-column sections with denser card grids. White and pale gray separate narratives; the ingredient section provides a stronger color change. Wide margins and large image surfaces do much of the visual work.

**Typography.** Large editorial headings lead; supporting text stays comparatively quiet. The adaptation substitutes Graymatter’s canonical Exposure display face and ABC Monument Grotesk Mono text face, retaining the contrast between expressive headlines and precise supporting information.

**Images.** The essential roles are a campaign hero, product-in-context image, athlete portrait, ingredient still lifes, individual product cutouts, and a science/support image. The build uses original assets for each role. It does not reuse Magic Mind imagery, logos, celebrity endorsement, or product-specific claims.

**Components.** Repeated pill CTAs, thin rules, split compositions, and aligned product cards are the reusable layout ingredients. Graymatter’s preference for restrained surfaces and no loud gradients overrides the reference’s colorful gradient treatment. A simple circle replaces the scalloped portrait edge.

## Brand rules used

The older general guidelines page contains unfinished sections. The more complete **Graymatter Brand Intake** and its September 9 voice update govern this concept.

| Semantic token | Value | Applied to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| surface.base | #FFFFFF | Default page and product grid |
| surface.inverse / text.primary | #161616 | Hero, final CTA section, dark text |
| surface.subtle | #F5F5F5 | Athlete section, cards, footer |
| surface.cool | #CDD9E8 | Ingredient section |
| surface.accent / text.highlight | #E6FDA5 | Selected CTA fills and limited dark-surface headline emphasis |
| text.inverse | #FFFFFF | Text on dark surfaces |

- Headlines use sentence case. Product names retain their proper capitalization.
- The established tagline retains its documented case exception.
- No em dashes or exclamation marks in authored page copy.
- Original logo file; no retyped or altered mark.
- High-contrast monochrome people imagery; restrained product compositions.
- No fabricated athlete quotation, medical board, clinical-trial result, or performance statistic.
- Benefit statements carry the single-asterisk convention and boxed disclaimer.

## Repeatable adaptation process

1. Inspect the rendered reference in the browser, including the full scroll, mobile stacking, disclosures, media, and CTA destinations.
2. Map section order, visual proportions, image roles, and what each section helps the visitor decide.
3. Read current brand tokens, typography, image rules, and copy rules. Resolve conflicting versions before authoring.
4. Match real brand assets to the reference’s image roles. Generate an asset only when suitable originals are unavailable, and label remaining gaps.
5. Write brand-specific copy from supported facts. Keep third-party claims and endorsements out of the adaptation.
6. Build locally and check images, typography, mobile overflow, keyboard interactions, and links.
7. Replace concept-only media/copy and resolve the documented review items before a separate publishing request.
